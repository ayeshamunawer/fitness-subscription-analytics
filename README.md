Subscription Analytics Project

Project Overview

This project analyzes customer subscription data to better understand customer retention, future subscription revenue, and the relationship between Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). The analysis was completed in Tableau.

Customer Cohort Analysis

The customer cohort analysis shows that customers typically cancel their subscriptions around Month 2. There is a significant decrease in the number of active customers around this point, suggesting that the first few months of the subscription are especially important for customer retention.

Key Finding: Customers typically cancel around 2 months after starting their subscription.

Revenue Forecast

Based on historical subscription revenue, Tableau forecasts that monthly revenue will increase over the next 12 months.

The forecast begins at approximately $235,088 in February 2026 and increases to approximately $320,616 by January 2027.

The forecast shows an overall upward trend in subscription revenue. There does not appear to be strong evidence of seasonality because the forecast increases fairly steadily rather than showing a clear repeating seasonal pattern.

Key Findings:

February 2026 forecast: $235,088

January 2027 forecast: $320,616

Overall trend: Increasing

Strong evidence of seasonality: No

CAC vs. LTV Analysis

The CAC vs. LTV analysis compares cumulative customer lifetime value with the total customer acquisition cost for the 2024 and 2025 customer cohorts.

The 2024 cohort reaches the CAC break-even point at approximately Month 1, meaning cumulative LTV has recovered the acquisition cost by that point.

The 2025 cohort reaches the CAC break-even point at Month 0, indicating that this cohort recovers its acquisition cost very quickly.

Key Findings:

2024 cohort CAC break-even: Month 1

2025 cohort CAC break-even: Month 0

Overall, the 2025 cohort reaches the CAC break-even point sooner than the 2024 cohort.
